# NeuroVue Privacy Policy

_Effective 20 November 2026_

NeuroVue is a viewer for neuroimaging data on macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone and
iPad. It is built to keep your data on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. NeuroVue has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or
tracking, and it does not send your files or usage data to us or to anyone else.

## Your files

Files you open stay on your device. NeuroVue reads them where they are, or keeps
a working copy in its own app storage, which is removed with the app.

## Sending between your devices

Send to iPad moves a file from a Mac to an iPhone or iPad on the same local
network. It happens only when you start it. The receiving device must turn on
receiving and accept each transfer, and both screens show the same code. The
transfer goes directly between the two devices and never through a server. It is
not encrypted, so use it only on networks you trust.

## AI agents (desktop)

"Allow AI Agents" is off by default. When you turn it on, AI tools on the same
computer (MCP clients) can list and open datasets in folders you allow, read
their metadata, change the view and capture images of it. NeuroVue itself sends
nothing off your computer, but the agent you connect may send what it reads to
its AI provider under that provider's own terms. Turn it off at any time in the
View menu.

## Sharing

When you share or export, NeuroVue hands the file to your system's share sheet
or save dialog. Where it goes from there is your choice.

## Children

NeuroVue does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any update to this policy is posted on this page with a new effective date.
